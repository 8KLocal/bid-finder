Sources, payment, and mail

It's Workplace (ITS WORKPLACE INC) publishes a partial list of public United States solicitations. This is not every federal, state, county, or city bid, and it is not a BidNet or RFPMart replacement. Each source card on the home page says when it was last read and how many notices we kept. A jurisdiction counts as covered only after its public bid page answered and its robots.txt allowed that fetch.

What we store

For each notice we keep a title, agency, place, dates, a short snippet, and a link to the official page. We do not copy bid packets, plans, or paywalled documents. SAM.gov rows come from the public Contract Opportunities CSV. Other rows come from public RSS, open-data files, or pages whose robots.txt allowed the request. Commercial portals that disallow crawling, or whose terms we could not confirm, are listed as skipped.

Alerts

Bid email is optional. We send a confirmation first. Every message has a one-click unsubscribe. The footer includes ITS WORKPLACE INC, 1261 Broadway, 2FL Suite IT, New York, NY 10001. It's Workplace is our offshore team handling the business.

AI bid writing — $20

AI writing is $20 per bid, charged by Stripe for It's Workplace, unless a white-label tenant sets a different price. Grok writes a draft only after Stripe confirms payment. The draft is a starting point for human review. It is not legal advice, it has not been submitted, and payment is not a guarantee of award. Our team reviews the draft before you treat it as finished.

Mail it for me

Mailing is a separate payment. We quote postage, a per-page amount, and our service fee from the rates configured for the tenant. A letter is sent only after Stripe confirms that payment. Notarized forms still need a third-party notary before you upload them. We do not guarantee a delivery time or that the agency will accept the packet. Deadlines are yours.

Takedown

If a notice should not be listed, email legal@itsworkplace.com with the page URL and the official source URL. We will remove the row from the next refresh. You can also write to ITS WORKPLACE INC, 1261 Broadway, 2FL Suite IT, New York, NY 10001.
