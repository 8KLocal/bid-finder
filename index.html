Official feeds

Federal notices come from the SAM.gov public contract-opportunity extract. Local notices come from Suffolk County’s Bonfire RSS and the NYC City Record. Each card links to the official page.

Deadlines kept as published

The feed’s own close text is stored. If a note disagrees with that clock, the conflict is flagged. Solicitation PDFs are not parsed automatically.

What is not covered

BidNet, RFPMart, PlanetBids, and other login portals are listed as unsupported or manual. A link to those sites is not treated as coverage.
