How it works and pricing

Find open US bids free. Pay only for a draft or a mailing.

It's Workplace lists public United States contract notices and refreshes them daily. Browsing is free. It's Workplace is our offshore team that runs this business.

01

Find open bids

The list is free. It includes federal, state, county, and city notices from the public sources we can read, and it is refreshed daily. This is not every US bid. Each row opens here and links to the original notice.

02

Save bids

Save to my bids keeps a notice on this device and opens a draft you can edit, copy, or download. Add your email if you want our team to reach out. Nothing is sent to a contracting officer unless you submit it yourself.

03

$20 AI fill-out

A written bid is $20, charged by Stripe. Grok writes the draft only after that payment. Our It's Workplace offshore team then reviews it and finishes it. The draft is a starting point. We do not submit it to a government portal.

04

Mail it for me

Mailing is optional. The quote is postage, a flat $5 mailing charge, then per-page printing. Stripe charges that total. Nothing is mailed until payment is confirmed. A notary, when one is required, is still yours to arrange.

Questions

Where do the bids come from?

From public sources: the SAM.gov public extract, public open-data files, and official pages whose robots.txt allowed the fetch. We do not copy paywalled packets. The home page lists each source and when it was last read.

What do you not guarantee?

We do not guarantee an award, revenue, a delivery date, or that an agency will accept a packet. Deadlines are yours. Always confirm the notice on the original page.

Refunds and contact

Refund and contact details are on the sources, payment, mail, and takedown page, including legal@itsworkplace.com and ITS WORKPLACE INC, 1261 Broadway, 2FL Suite IT, New York, NY 10001.
